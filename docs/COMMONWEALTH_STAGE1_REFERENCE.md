# The Defence of the Commonwealth — Stage 1 reference extract

Source: the user-supplied **Prologue Campaign — Design Reference v0.1**, November
2026, read in full before implementation. This file preserves its Stage 1 scope
and cast; it is not a replacement narrative or an implementation of the campaign.
The source's numerical balance remains provisional, so new profiles use existing
simulation values as baselines. Battlefield abilities are fictional alternate-history
design choices, not historical claims about the individuals.

## Battlefield identities from Sections 3–4

- Commonwealth: flexible combined arms; regular infantry, citizen defenders,
  melee-only Scythemen and different cavalry roles. Lancers emphasize one initial
  impact rather than repeated impact or universally strong sustained melee.
- Revolutionary Britain: flexible volley infantry and slower-firing, longer-ranged
  rifles. Existing terrain and line-of-sight rules remain authoritative.
- Haudenosaunee: small, mobile detachments with woodland advantages and useful
  open-ground harassment/disengagement. No invented cultural abilities, naming
  conventions or stereotyped models.
- Prussia: cohesive firing lines, elite assault infantry and less agile heavy
  cavalry. No unconditional faction-wide bonus.
- Russia: staying power, offensive infantry and irregular light horse; resilience
  is not immunity to morale shocks.

## Preserved boundaries

Faction identifies a regiment; army identifies battlefield allegiance. Alliances
belong in scenario coalitions, not universal combat rules. Historical identity is
separate from generic generation and acquired upgrades. Named build combinations
must emerge from ordinary mechanics, not hardcoded sets of units.

The six campaign missions, branching events, persistent casualties, recruitment,
politics, upgrades/rewards, fortifications, campaign saving and endings remain
Stage 2 or later work. Tatar cavalry, Scouts, Fusiliers, engineers, experimental
shells, Royalist/EIC formations and the other 16 proposed archetypes are not added.
